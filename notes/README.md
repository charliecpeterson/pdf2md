# Field reports

Written by consumers of pdf2md, not by its maintainer, after using it for real
work. They report what happened on one workload with paths and numbers, and they
are kept verbatim — including the parts where a report was wrong, and the
corrections written into it afterwards.

They are here because several design decisions in this project exist only
because of them, and a decision is easier to judge next to the evidence that
prompted it. `CLAUDE.md` records what was changed; these say why anyone looked.

- `2026-08-28-field-report-claim-checking.md` — checking citations in papers
  under review. About prose and whether a claim survives a second reading.
- `2026-09-05-field-report-ecpgen-numeric.md` — taking numbers out of a paper
  and into a fit. About tables, equations and metadata, and the one that found
  a wrong basis-set exponent that had passed every structural gate.

The corpus behind the first report quotes source text that is not ours to
redistribute, so its harness and labels stay local; the report itself is public.
